His ideas

Three ideas anchor his work

Each one shows up in how Kapunga was built.

As farmers grow, we grow

A business serving farmers succeeds only when its farmers succeed. Shared prosperity is the commercial model, not a side project.

Seeing is believing

Farmers adopt new practices when they see them work on a neighbour's field. Demonstration persuades where instruction cannot.

Ubuntu

"I am because we are." Agricultural transformation is a shared undertaking between farmers, communities, business and the state.
