"Farmers are not the problem. The fragmented ecosystem surrounding them is the problem."

The central argument

Africa's smallholders work hard on good land. What holds them back is the system around them: inputs, agronomy, irrigation, finance, mechanisation, processing and markets that rarely arrive together.

The book argues that when these are connected inside a commercially sustainable system, the farmer prospers, and so does the business that serves them.
